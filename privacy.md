# Privacy policy: Margin Notes factory

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Margin Notes factory is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Margin Notes. It signs in with Google to upload videos, thumbnails, captions and translations to that one channel, manage its playlists and comments, and read its YouTube Analytics.

What it accesses: only the Margin Notes channel's own YouTube data, through the YouTube Data, Analytics and Reporting APIs, with the owner's consent.

What it stores: the sign-in token and the channel's own analytics, kept on the owner's computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data about viewers or anyone else is collected.

How to revoke access: remove "Margin Notes factory" at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions at any time.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
