# Margin Notes factory

Margin Notes factory is the private production tool behind the YouTube channel [Margin Notes](https://www.youtube.com/@MarginNotes-k6s), which explains the economics of everyday things.

It is used only by the channel's owner to upload videos, captions and translations to that channel and to read its analytics. It is not a public app and has no other users.
